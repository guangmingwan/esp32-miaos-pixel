# fMSX licensing and BIOS policy

This app vendors unmodified fMSX 6.0 by Marat Fayzullin from the retro-go
fMSX component. The authoritative terms remain in
`vendor/fmsx/src/fMSX/fMSX.html`. fMSX is not public domain and its source may
not be used for commercial purposes. Credit and the original conditions must
be preserved when copying or modifying it. This restriction applies even when
the surrounding launcher is distributed under the GPL.

No MSX BIOS, ROM, disk, or other copyrighted machine asset is included. Users
must legally provide all ten documented BIOS files in `/bios/msx` and media in
`/roms/msx`. The app validates the complete BIOS set before starting the core.
